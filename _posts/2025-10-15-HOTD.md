## What is House of the Dead Remake
In short HotDR is reinvention of classic of arcade games - the ones where you shoot with plastic pistol as a controller pointing it at screen. It's a very retro railshooter with multiple paths to take and many bosses to fight.

## My participation
Since project was heavily into production my help consistent mostly of creating tooling and figuring well working audio system for the game for positional audio with FMOD. Further into the project - since I've been very accustomed to the internals of FMOD engine - I've participated in patches for the game for various platforms. (Playstation, XBOX, Switch)

## Biggest successes:
- Stable audio system
- Tool for physics layer management for all scenes
- Sucessfull patches

## Biggest regrets
- Didn't get to work on a game from the very beggining
