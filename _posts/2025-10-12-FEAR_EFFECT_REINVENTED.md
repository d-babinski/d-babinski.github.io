### What is Fear Effect

Fear Effect Reinvented (originally made by Kronos Digital/Eidos) is a remake project of a Play Station 1  horror game with static screens (think old Resident Evils) with a whole game remade to adjust to modern similar games (think Resident Evil remakes). Game was fully remade from scratch for 3rd person perspective which meant redesigning literally every aspect of a game.

### My Timeline
(March 2021 - July 2023)
I've joined the project around 1-1.5 years into the production when only one developer was working on the game. We've done preproduction of a game for about 1 year before more resources could be assigned to a project. Over time team rose to 7 game developers (most of them were juniors which needed guidance). Last half year of my participation in the project we were back to 2 developers trying to tie up the ends of the game while also porting it to all platforms (Switch/PS4/5/XBOXOne). Project came to end 3 months after my departure from company

### Personal Achievements

The things I am most proud of:
- Learning - I've started the project as a fresh entry to the gamedev with just few gamejams and project behind the belt and I've learnt incredible amount of things in a very enjoyable enviroment
- Spatial Audio - I've developed and optimized a way to handle ambiences and filters in a mixed outdoor/indoor enviroments with directional and spatialized audios and dynamic changes (doors getting open/closed, walls getting blown). System was very easy to use and very fun to work with. My main inspirations were spatial audio in new Hitman Games and Quantum Break/Control games. 
- Big systems - I've designed/implemented from scratch whole custom segments like:
  -  Enemies driving and driveby shooting a train in a 6 min segment (pooling, spawn points/roads, destruction, behaviors, driving model)
  -  AI pack behaviour - our units were choosing to be Defensive/Aggressive and choosing their positions/decisions based on that and combat space
  -  UI - I have handled all the UI in the game including main menus,inventories,puzzles, settings, input remapping etc. and everything included in a good interface(sounds, animations, shaders)
  -  NPC Behaviours - I was handling most of AI and mechanics related to gameplay to the point of designing most of tasks/behaviour trees/special cases as well as bosses(which as you can probably imagine - were fully custom). Had a lot of fun, big learning curve but very enjoyable.

These are just few in a sea of things I was involved in.

### Regrets
My main regrets are:
- Overachieveing and focusing too much on form - I guess this is just bound to inexperienced ambitious people - we want things to be too perfect instead of just good enough. It was great lesson learnt.
- Game didnt get to be released - since it was first non-direct remake and complete revamp of the game studio didn't really have experience in directing such a big (7-8 hours of gameplay) project, this meant usuall delays in planning were much bigger and it was way harder to assume the level of completion
- Losing the teammates - team was honestly great and I'm really sad I don't get to work with each one of them on daily basis
