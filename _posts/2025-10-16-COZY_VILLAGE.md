## What is Thrivelands?
Thrivelands is my solo project made in Godot. It's a  roguelike citybuilder on a hexagonal grid where you play cards to gain coins and spend coins for bonuses/new cards while exploring the map.
The general flow of the game can be caught in the short video below:
{% include embed/youtube.html id='MNXl2ip4zQ4' %}

This was born initially as a gamejam project, getting good feedback about being fun which made decision to try to shape into real full game an easy task. Game is currently in progress and will be soon available on Steam.

## What I'm proud of:
- Having well thought out data structures, adding, removing and changing things in the game is a blast. At no time do I really need to think much about where to put things as it occurs more naturally.
- Only a bit abstraction where it's needed. This time i really tried to let abstraction develop over time and it pays off.
- Good looking pixelart - long years of practice have paid off.
- Simple but extensible design - most of things are done via cards/buttons and quests meaning I have rigid system of creating complex mechanics with these tools.
- It's fun. Playtesters confirm the execution of idea is quite addicting.

## What I'm concerned about:
- Sometimes I still overengineer solutions althought to much much lesser extent.
- I still have a lot to learn about game design.
- Solo game dev is a lot of work.
