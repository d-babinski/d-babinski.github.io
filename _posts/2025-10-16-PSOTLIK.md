## What is Psotlik?.

Psotlik is my tribute to the games and genres I love which are soulslikes, old darkfantasy rpgs while maintaning ghibli atmosphere of magic and forest spirits. (And big influence of Mushishi). Idea has started to shape long ago during my begginings of game development and my first game jam slowly and slowly taking shape into fully fledged game.
Similar to many big projects made by a single dev who's learning over time - I've rebuilt game several times each time with more knowledge(I'm jealous of people who didn't face that) eventually  for project to be put on hiatus while I proceed with smaller game to get my foot into the indie game development scene.

## What I enjoyed about project:
- It took big part into my maturing as a develeoper and creator
- It was a big reminder of why I fell in love with game dev in a first place
- It shows a lot of potential -  visible by my reach on social platforms (40 thousand people on one post!)
- It has a lot of moving parts to play around with and a huge design space
- Learning a lot about myself and about what workflows work and which don't

## What are my regrets:
- I've not limited the scope of the game on time,
- Approaching such a huge design challenge without that much of design experience
- Being unrealistically ambitious without realistic plan
